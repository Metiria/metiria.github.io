Sergio Giuliano

Drums

Catania, Italy

Music Education

Sergio Giuliano (born in Catania, August 30, 2009) discovered his vocation for music at an early age while listening to Fabrizio de Andrè with his father and started taking drum lessons at the age of 5

In 2025 Enzo Augello became his teacher and mentor. Under his guidance, Sergio is preparing himself for theadmission to the S. Arlia (ex P.I. Tchaikovsky) State Conservatory in Nocera Terinese (CZ).

Bands and Projects

Sergio Giuliano has played drums in various bands with different backgrounds and styles.

After co-founding the funk/soul project Astronauts, he joined the alternative rock band Beautiful Mother Mary in 2025 and became an effective member of the progressive rock and alternative metal band Metiria in 2026.

Inspirations

His musical identity is influenced by his listenings inherent to national and international pop rock and progressive rock.

Bands & Artists: Genesis, PFM, Pooh, Elio e le Storie Tese and Claudio Baglioni.

Drummers: Gavin Harrison, Phil Collins, Christian Meyer, Franz di Cioccio and Elio Rivagli.

Style

His approach to drumming is directed to being at the service of the song and the band, trying to stand out only when needed.

His style aims to blend technique and dynamics especially while using asymmetrical time signatures.

Other Relevant Information

In addition to his passion for music, he has a particular interest in cinema and is owner of a vaste vinyl collection.
