Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

At Metiria, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us through our website.

A. Types of Data Collected

We collect personal and technical information necessary to provide you with the best fan experience, including:

  • Personal Identifiers: Names and email addresses (e.g., when signing up for our newsletter, contact forms, or fan club).
  • Technical & Network Data: IP addresses, browser types, and device information.
  • Usage Data & Cookies: Pages visited, interaction with media player content, and preference settings via web cookies.

B. Purpose of Data Collection

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes, such as:

  • Delivering band updates, tour announcements, news, and promotional communications.
  • Analyzing website traffic and usage patterns to optimize performance and user experience.
  • Ensuring technical security and maintaining legal and regulatory compliance.

C. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers (such as email newsletter services, website hosting, and analytics tools) strictly under secure conditions required to operate this service.

D. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as rights under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data held by us.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in our newsletter.

E. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with applicable legal obligations.
