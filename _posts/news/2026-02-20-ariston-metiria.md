**The band from Catania, with a sound that blends prog, pop openings, and contemporary metal, from their debut at the Benedictine Monastery to third place at Sanremo Rock and the semifinal of the San Marino Song Contest.**

**From the cellars of Catania to the big stages: the rise of Metiria** A sonic imprint that blends **prog influences, pop openings, and the drive of contemporary metal**. This is the hallmark of **Metiria**, a band from Catania consisting of **Aurora Amas (vocals)**, **Samuele Mangiaratti (drums)**, **Lorenzo Ragusa (bass)**, **Angelo Li Rosi (keyboards)** from Carlentini, and **Arturo Caflisch (guitar)**.

The group finds its **definitive line-up in 2024**, the year of their **official debut at the Benedictine Monastery**.

The roots of the project, however, date back a few years earlier, inside an **independent studio** built by the musicians themselves: between **intense rehearsals** and **late-night experimentations**, a **precise identity** takes shape, the result of **research** and **targeted influences**.

**The first live shows** take place in **local venues across the province**, between **contests and small theaters**, essential steps to **refine their chemistry and stage presence**.

Little by little, the **horizon expands beyond regional borders**, giving substance to steady growth.

The turning point comes with **Sanremo Rock**: after passing the regional and interregional selections, **Metiria** land in the **Grand Final on the stage of the Ariston Theater**, securing **third place overall**.

A result that counts as both an **artistic recognition** and a powerful **driver of visibility**.

In the wake of that experience, the group is offered the chance to participate in the **San Marino Song Contest**: out of **over 800 candidates** at the castings, the five manage to make it into the **top 240**, accessing the **Stage & Live Academy** phase and competing against artists from all over Italy and abroad.

Today **Metiria** have been selected for the **first semifinal**, broadcast **live on Rtv San Marino on March 4th**: they are among the **20 chosen names**, out of **40 in the competition**, battling for access to the grand final.

A journey built with **determination**, born in the **heart of Catania** and matured without shortcuts, supported by **daily work**, **discipline**, and a **clear musical vision**.
