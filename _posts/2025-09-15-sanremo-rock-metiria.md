**"LIOTRU" BRAND. After reaching the national finals, the five young artists secured third place overall.**

A major achievement for a "Liotru" band, which took the stage at Sanremo’s renowned Ariston Theatre this weekend to compete in the national finals of "Sanremo Rock."

The band is Metiria, a symphonic rock group from Catania with strong influences drawn from modern progressive metal.
The ensemble consists of five members: Aurora Amas (vocals), Giorgia Cristina De Pasquale (keyboards), Samuele Mangiaratti (drums), Lorenzo Ragusa (bass), and Arturo Caflisch (guitar). Over the weekend, the group carved out a significant niche for themselves—and perhaps caught the eye of a major producer—during the event; they first reached the grand final and then, competing against Italy's top six bands, secured third place overall in the competition.

The band fully formed in Catania in 2024 with the aim of experimenting with fresh sounds, utilizing cutting-edge instrumentation—such as eight- and twelve-string guitars, six-string basses, acoustic and electronic percussion, pianos, and keyboards—to complement the frontwoman's rich, powerful vocals. The intricate rhythms characteristic of modern extreme genres provide a counterpoint to powerful melodies, supported by harmonies that range from classical counterpoint to modern modal music. The group’s multifaceted artistic vision stems from the members' diverse stylistic backgrounds, which span pop-rock, classical music, jazz, funk, and metal. The sound they developed—already clearly evident in their first original tracks, *Attention Seeker*, *Jura*, and *State of Mind* (all set to appear on their debut album, currently being mastered)—finds its full expression in the song "Sorrow." This track immediately stood out among the entries in a competition held at the Auditorium Gervasio in Matera.

It was this very song—"Sorrow"—that was submitted to the "Sanremo Rock" contest, leading to Metiria being selected from hundreds of Sicilian bands to advance directly to the regional finals held in Noto last August. After securing a top-three finish and qualifying for the inter-regional finals (covering Campania, Basilicata, Calabria, Puglia, and Sicily), Metiria successfully made the cut as one of the eight finalists representing Southern Italy at the contest's national finals.
